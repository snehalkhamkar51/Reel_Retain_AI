    How ReelRetain AI analyzes your reel
    1. Measured properties: local video extraction records duration, sampled scene changes, motion, available audio features, and supplied caption counts.
    2. AI-generated content analysis: optional Gemini analysis describes the video only after explicit consent; its observations and timestamp suggestions are not independently verified.
    3. Experimental ML predictions: the existing model estimates retention-related outcomes from synthetic training patterns; Gemini does not supply or change those numbers.
    4. Verified external metrics: none are retrieved. User-provided reference URLs are shown as unverified links only.
    5. Hypotheses and guidance: audience hypotheses and editing suggestions are ideas to test, not observed viewer behavior.
    Analysis ID Estimates are not a measure of future audience behavior.
