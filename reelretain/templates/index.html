    A CLEARER PROCESS, NOT A CRYSTAL BALL

    From first frame
    to thoughtful next step.

    ReelRetain turns a draft into a handful of measurable signals and experimental model outputs. You stay in charge of the edit.

    01

    Share a draft

    Choose a short video and optionally add its caption. No social login or connected account required.

    02

    Measure the signals

    The local service extracts video metadata, sampled-frame changes, motion, audio where available, and caption counts.

    03

    Explore the estimates

    Three regression pipelines return clearly labeled synthetic estimates alongside the measured features and limitations.

    04

    Make it yours

    Use conditional, feature-based prompts to review the edit. Your judgment—and real audience evidence—still matters.
